01 / ABOUT SVARA

Music should be felt, not just taught.

Svara Music Academy is a home-based learning space built around a simple idea: give students practical guidance, individual attention and the confidence to actually make music.

Our classes focus on Guitar and Basic Vocals, with learning structured into clear levels so students can see their progress.
